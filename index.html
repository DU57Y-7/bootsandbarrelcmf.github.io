THE INAUGURAL FESTIVAL · LOGAN, QUEENSLAND

One night.
All country.

Boots & Barrel Country Music Festival is coming to Logan, Queensland on Saturday 22 May 2027, bringing Australian country music, country culture and an unforgettable festival atmosphere to the region.

We're building a full festival experience with live performances, emerging talent, food trucks, country market stalls and entertainment. We're targeting approximately 2,000 festivalgoers for our first year.

Artist announcements, ticketing details and the full program will be released as arrangements are confirmed.

Boots & Barrel is presented by Razor Blade Productions (QLD) Pty Ltd. Meet the team behind the festival ↗
