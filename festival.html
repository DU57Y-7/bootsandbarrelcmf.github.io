Event Info

Country music is coming to Logan

Boots & Barrel Country Music Festival is a brand-new event currently in development, bringing Australian country music, live entertainment and community together for May 2027.

Our vision

A standout community experience

The proposed festival is being developed around an eight-hour live music program on a Saturday, with an anticipated audience of 2,000+ attendees.

We aim to celebrate Australian country music while creating a welcoming space for fans, artists and the wider community. We also see opportunities for local businesses to connect with an engaged audience and contribute to a new event in the region.

Live country music

Celebrating the sound and spirit of Australian country music.

Community connection

Bringing people together around a shared love of live entertainment.

Local opportunity

Creating opportunities for businesses, suppliers and supporters to be part of the journey.

Currently in development. The festival is subject to final approvals. The exact date, venue and program have not been announced.
